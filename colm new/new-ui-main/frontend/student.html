SUPPORT

Help & FAQs

Answers to common questions about requesting documents.

How do I request a document?

Open Document Catalog, choose a document, upload every required file, then submit. The system checks your files and the Registrar reviews them.

How are my documents checked?

Every file is checked when you upload it: it must be a supported file type, readable, complete, the right document, and show your name. If it is declined you will see the reason and can upload a clearer or correct copy. The Registrar still reviews and approves your documents.

What if the Registrar asks for a correction?

You will get a notification. Open My Requests, click View, and upload the corrected files.

How do I pay?

Pay in person at the registrar office. The cashier will mark your request as paid and you will be notified.

Who can I contact?

Visit the Registrar's Office during office hours, or check the Organizational Chart for the staff in charge of your program.
